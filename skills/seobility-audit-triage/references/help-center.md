# Seobility Help Center articles per report

Every Website Audit report has a Help Center article in German, English and Spanish that explains
what the report checks, why it matters and how to fix it. **These articles are the reference for
every explanation and recommendation this skill gives.** The skill adds what the crawl says about
this particular site; the article supplies what the problem is and how it is fixed.

Mapping verified on 23.09.2026: all 177 URLs below returned HTTP 200. The articles live on
`help.seobility.net`; slugs differ per language, so never build a URL by translating a slug - use
this table.

## Which language

Link the article in the language the user writes in: German `de`, English `en`, Spanish `es`. For
any other language, link the English article.

## How to use an article

1. **Before writing a group's explanation, read its article** (web fetch, if the environment has
   it). Take the explanation of the problem, the reason it matters and the recommended fix from
   there, in plain words, and add what the crawl showed on this site. Keep the article's concrete
   criteria (character ranges, thresholds) instead of inventing your own.
2. **Where the article and this skill's fix shape disagree, the article wins** on what the problem
   is and how it is fixed. The skill's own rules still decide the order and the effort on this
   site (template or per page, reach, rankings). Follow the article in the output; a disagreement
   is a bug in `impact-effort.md`, not something to explain to the reader.
3. **Link it** at the end of each group, each "later" line and each "not worth changing" line,
   with the article's title as the link text: `More in the Help Center: [Duplicate page titles](…)`, in the user's language.
4. Without web access, still link the article and write the explanation from
   `impact-effort.md`.

Do not quote the articles at length. Paraphrase in the reader's words.

Two articles are easy to mix up:

- `pages-with-duplicate-paragraphs` ("Pages with duplicate paragraphs") means the **same
  paragraph repeated on one page**, often a box the layout outputs twice.
- `pages-with-duplicate-text-content` ("Content that appears on several pages") means the **same
  text on several pages**. Notes and disclaimers that appear on very many pages are left out of
  that report by Seobility itself.
